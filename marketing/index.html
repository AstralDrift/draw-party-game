How it plays

One TV hosts the show. Phones draw, invent titles, and vote — same Party Glass UI you get in the app.

    Draw the prompt

    Fool the couch with convincing guesses while someone else’s art is on the TV.

  3. 3

    Vote for the truth

    Lettered titles on the TV. Tap the line you think is real on your phone.

Living-room scale

Calm frames, generous space — the TV carries the show; phones stay out of the way.
